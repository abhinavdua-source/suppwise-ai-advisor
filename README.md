Google Stitch Prompt – AI Supplement & Fitness Advisor

Project Overview

Design a modern mobile application called SuppWise (placeholder name) that acts as an AI-powered fitness and supplement advisor. The app should help users understand why they are experiencing a fitness or wellness issue before recommending evidence-based supplements. The experience should feel trustworthy, minimal, scientific, and premium rather than like an e-commerce or influencer platform.

The design language should be inspired by Apple Health, Whoop, Notion, and Perplexity AI, with a clean interface, generous white space, subtle gradients, rounded cards, modern typography, and intuitive navigation.

⸻

Primary User Journey

When users open the app, they should immediately see a search bar that asks:

“What are you struggling with today?”

Example queries:

* I feel tired all the time.
* I’m not recovering after workouts.
* I can’t lose fat.
* I’m sleeping badly.
* I have muscle cramps.
* My energy crashes during workouts.
* Which supplements should I take for muscle growth?

The AI should ask intelligent follow-up questions before providing recommendations.

Design Style

Premium.

Scientific.

Modern.

Rounded cards.

Large typography.

Soft gradients.

Blue, white, and emerald green color palette.

Avoid bright reds or flashy fitness aesthetics.

Icons should be simple and consistent.

Animations should be subtle and smooth.

⸻

UX Principles

* Prioritize education before recommendations.
* Show confidence levels instead of certainty.
* Clearly distinguish evidence-backed advice from emerging research.
* Present safety warnings and potential interactions prominently.
* Make the experience conversational, trustworthy, and easy to understand.
* Avoid an e-commerce-first feel; recommendations should appear unbiased and focused on helping users make informed decisions.

The final prototype should include polished onboarding, all primary screens, smooth navigation between them, realistic placeholder data, and a complete end-to-end user journey from asking a health question to receiving personalized, evidence-based guidance.
